# Authentic Contractor Copywriting & Prompting Engine

This skill provides a systematic protocol to generate, audit, and refine frontend website copy for trade contractors (flooring, roofing, remodeling, plumbing, electrical, painting). It guarantees that all text sounds 100% human, trustworthy, liability-safe, and grounded in real job-site realities rather than sounding like generic, AI-generated marketing fluff.

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy: The "Toolbelt Test"

> **The Toolbelt Test:**  
> If an experienced, honest tradesman would NEVER say a sentence out loud while standing in a homeowner's living room holding a tape measure, **do NOT write it on the website.**

### AI-Generated vs. Authentic Tradesman Comparison

| ❌ AI Cliché / Fluff / Unsafe Assumption (BANNED) | ✅ Authentic Tradesman Voice (MANDATORY) |
|---|---|
| *"Delve into an unparalleled symphony of bespoke flooring artistry."* | *"We install luxury vinyl plank (LVP), residential carpet, and commercial carpet tile that looks clean and lasts."* |
| *"100% waterproof rigid core guarantee that will never squeak or buckle."* | *"Durable, water-resistant vinyl planks designed for kitchens, bathrooms, and active households."* |
| *"We grind down all humps and self-level every subfloor for free."* | *"Subfloor Check: We inspect your subfloor flatness upfront and let you know if any leveling or dip patching is needed."* |
| *"Spotless turnkey living sanctuary."* | *"We clean up after our work every day and leave your room ready to use."* |
| *"From the first measurement to the final piece of trim."* | *"From the first measurement to the final walk-through."* |

## 2. The Zero-Assumption & Liability Guardrails

Contractor websites must NEVER make legally risky, exaggerated, or false scope assumptions:

1. 🚫 **No Absolute Guarantees:** Never say *"100% waterproof guarantee"* or *"floors will never squeak"*. Use honest, realistic descriptions: *"water-resistant vinyl plank"*, *"subfloor prep to minimize squeaks and movement"*.
2. 🚫 **No Assumed Free Extra Labor:** Subfloor leveling, major grinding, and self-leveling cement pours are paid remediation services. Present them as an **inspection/assessment step**: *"We check subfloor flatness and discuss leveling options if your subfloor has low dips."*
3. 🚫 **No Specific Brand/Spec Inventions:** Never fabricate specs like *"8-lb high-density pad"* or *"HEPA filtration"* unless explicitly confirmed by the contractor. Refer to *"quality underlayment pads"* and *"clean dust-controlled job sites"*.
4. 🚫 **No Confusing Phrasing or Jargon:** Avoid confusing words (e.g. "plus", "turnkey holistic", "micro-bevel tolerances"). Speak in clear, plain English.

---

## 3. The Banned Vocabulary Index

Whenever drafting or refining copy, automatically search for and replace these robotic words:

- 🚫 **Banned Verbs & Transitions:** *Delve, Elevate, Unlock, Transform (when overused), Synergize, Harness, Foster, Ensure seamless integration.*
- 🚫 **Banned Adjectives:** *Bespoke, Unparalleled, Meticulous, Quintessential, Holistic, Transformative, Unrivaled, Pristine, Exquisite, Harmonious, Spotless (sounds like maid service).*
- 🚫 **Banned Nouns:** *Sanctuary, Symphony, Tapestry, Paradigm, Realm, Pinnacle, Epitome, Haven, Journey (unless literally driving).*

---

## 4. Four Core Pillars of Contractor Website Copy

1. **Clear Scope Over Vague Claims:**
   - Clearly state what is installed: *Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP), Residential Carpet, Stair Runners, Commercial Carpet Tile*.
2. **Explain the Real Work (What Homeowners Actually Care About):**
   - Subfloor flatness checks, proper expansion perimeter spacing, door casing undercuts, and power stretching for carpets.
3. **Transparent 4-Step Process:**
   - **Step 1:** Call, text, or submit your room sizes online.
   - **Step 2:** We bring real plank & carpet samples to your house and take laser measurements.
   - **Step 3:** You get an honest, clear written estimate.
   - **Step 4:** We install on schedule and clean up our workspace.
4. **Local Identity & Personal Accountability:**
   - Mention the exact Triangle cities and zip codes served.
   - Use the owner's actual name and direct contact (*Call or text Randol Perez at (919) 473-6731*).

---

## 5. Automated Copy Review Checklist

Before approving any UI copy, verify:
- [ ] Are all absolute claims ("100%", "never squeaks", "spotless") removed?
- [ ] Is subfloor leveling framed as a thorough inspection & optional prep service rather than assumed free labor?
- [ ] Are all confusing words (like "plus") eliminated?
- [ ] Does it sound like an honest, experienced contractor talking directly to a homeowner?
- [ ] Is there a clear, low-friction next step (Call, text, or schedule free in-home measurement)?
