# Privacy Policy for NeverMiss.ai

**Last Updated:** July 17, 2026

NeverMiss.ai (operating under the name "NeverMiss.ai", also referenced in connection with the "SnapPicFix" integrations and services) is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, their clients, and callers. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered voicemail replacement, lead capture, and call routing services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide our voice intelligence, spam detection, and CRM integration services, we collect the following categories of information:

### A. Information Gathered from Callers and Leads
When a caller interacts with your NeverMiss.ai voicemail or AI agent, we process the call stream to extract:
*   **Caller Identity Details:** Full Name, Caller ID, and Phone Number.
*   **Property & Contact Address:** Standard mailing or service address details provided during the call.
*   **Call Metadata & Transcripts:** Full audio recordings, text-based speech-to-text transcripts, and semantic call summaries (e.g., job descriptions, call intent).
*   **Spam & Lead Salience Markers:** Indicators determining whether the caller is a genuine lead ("Hot Lead") or a telemarketer/spam call.

### B. Business Account Information
When you register a business number or onboarding profile with us, we collect:
*   **Business Details:** Business name, website URL, social media page listings (e.g., Facebook Page, Google Business Profile) used to train the underlying AI models.
*   **Account Contact Info:** Business email, phone number, and administrative login credentials.
*   **Third-Party API Access Keys & OAuth Tokens:** Authorized credentials required to sync lead records with your field management and CRM software.

---

## 2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected data to run, optimize, and secure the NeverMiss.ai services, specifically:
*   **Speech Processing & Information Extraction:** Converting voice streams to text transcripts and parsing structured fields (Name, Address, Description) using semantic models.
*   **Lead Classification & Triage:** Sorting incoming requests into priority queues (e.g., spam filtering vs. instant notification for high-intent leads).
*   **Automated Text Back & Messaging:** Dispatching automatic SMS follow-ups to missed calls and text-back responses.
*   **Model Training and Customization:** Contextualizing and training your specific AI agent using your website, facebook pages, or business directories.
*   **CRM Synchronization:** Pushing extracted lead contact files and audio transcripts directly into your CRM.

---

## 3. Storage and Data Retention

*   **Local Storage & Processing:** Active transcripts and audio files are securely processed on our cloud instances and cached to ensure fast pipeline execution and low turnaround latency.
*   **CRM Custody:** Once lead details are successfully synchronized with your target CRM (e.g., ServiceTitan, Jobber), the primary record of truth is transferred to and governed by your CRM provider's retention rules.
*   **Data Deletion:** Account holders can request deletion of caller history and training data at any time by contacting our support team.

---

## 4. Third-Party Integrations & Data Sharing

NeverMiss.ai integrates with and syncs customer records to the following third-party field service management and CRM platforms:
*   **ServiceTitan:** Creating records for new clients and appending work requests.
*   **Jobber:** Syncing new clients and recording incoming work requests.
*   **Networx & Angi:** Processing incoming lead notifications and dispatching automated replies.
*   **Thumbtack:** Replying instantly to weekend or off-hours leads.
*   **ServiceMinder, ClientTether, Leap CRM, DripJobs:** Exporting parsed client sheets and dispatching text follow-ups.

We do not sell, rent, or trade caller data to third-party advertisers. Data is shared with the above integrations solely at the direction and authorization of the account owner.

---

## 5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to secure voice data, API keys, and business credentials. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS, and database entries are isolated using row-level security (RLS) policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our telephony integrations or AI pipelines. The "Last Updated" date at the top will indicate when changes have been made.
